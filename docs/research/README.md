# Research logs

Research logs are exploratory. They are not decisions.

## What belongs where

- **ADR**: a decision that constrains future work.
- **Spec**: the current design of the product, schema, or engine.
- **Research log**: a dated record of experiments, critiques, and open questions.
- **Idea**: an unscheduled concept.
- **Transcript**: raw conversation, if kept, marked non-authoritative.

Do not put a raw chat into an ADR. An ADR should state the decision and its consequences, not replay the discussion.
