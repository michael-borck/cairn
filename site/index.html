Why Cairn

Adaptation you can own and inspect.

Most adaptive platforms own the path, the content, and the data. Cairn takes the other position.

Private by default

No account. Learner data lives in a local SQLite database. AI off is a complete mode, not a degraded one.

Your material

Import Markdown, HTML, DOCX, EPUB, and PDF. Bring Docker labs, lecture notes, and textbooks. Build and export packs.

Visible adaptation

Every activity has a reason. Change difficulty, help, AI, or path. Switch to manual mode when you want the wheel.

Status

Pilot-ready, not finished.

Cairn v0.2 is a research-grade prototype. The adaptive model, retention controls, packs, authoring, and local export are working. A public pilot and a signed release are next. The adaptive methods are inherited from established work. The local-first, learner-owned workflow is the contribution.
